EverydayImagesCompression

Image to target file size

A form needs a smaller photo? Choose your image, enter the maximum file size and download a JPG that fits.

Your photo is processed only in your browser. It is never uploaded to a server.

JPG, PNG or WebP. One photo at a time. You can also drop it here.

1 KB = 1,000 bytes. 1 MB = 1,000 KB.

Result

Download JPG

The tool checks the actual file size, first adjusting JPG quality and then reducing dimensions if needed. It never enlarges, crops or stretches your photo. Transparency becomes white. A JPG already within the limit is offered unchanged. HEIC is not supported; export it as JPG first. Meeting the file-size limit does not guarantee that a form will accept the photo.
