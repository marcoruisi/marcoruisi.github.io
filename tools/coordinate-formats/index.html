Coordinate formats

Convert latitude and longitude between decimal degrees, degrees and decimal minutes, and degrees minutes seconds.

CRS converter · Points on a map · GIS data at points

DMS to decimal degrees, and back

This changes coordinate notation, not the reference system. Coordinates are WGS84 geographic (EPSG:4326), not UTM. Use the CRS converter to change projection.

Accepts DD: 44.4866667, DMM: 44° 29.2′ N, DMS: 44° 29′ 12″ N. Also S/W, negative signs and decimal commas.

Limits: latitude ±90° and longitude ±180°. Rounded output (DD 8 decimals, DMM 6 decimals, DMS 2 decimals).
