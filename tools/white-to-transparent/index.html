White to transparent

Remove a white background from a logo or image and download a transparent PNG. Local, free processing.

Your file stays in your browser: nothing is uploaded to a server.

PNG, JPG, WebP · Up to 16 million pixels.

The preview background is not exported. The optional white shape is part of the PNG. Interior whites are preserved in edge mode.

Designed for logos and images on a plain white background, not automatic removal of photographic backgrounds. Check light details before downloading.
