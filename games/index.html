How offline games work

Sudoku, Crossword, 2048 and the other MRC games run in your browser. To use them without the internet, open this page while online and wait for “Games ready for airplane mode.” Your browser saves the pages and files needed on this device.

Once preparation is complete, you can turn on airplane mode, reopen games, switch between them and reload pages without a connection. No app to install. No account needed.

Preparation is needed separately for each device and browser. Clearing site data or automatic browser storage cleanup means you will need to prepare again while online. External links and contributions still need the internet. And remember: these games are for a short break, not hours in front of a screen.
