Contact

If you’ve made it this far, you were probably looking for more than a portfolio.

Perhaps you’re interested in the same kinds of questions.

How can a system be made simpler?

How can complexity be reduced without losing functionality?

How can different tools work better together?

Or perhaps you simply came across an idea that made you look at a familiar problem in a different way.

Either way, I’d be happy to hear from you.

I don’t promise to have the right answer.

The best conversations, after all, almost always begin with a good question.

If you think there's a conversation worth having, you can reach me here.

If you're trying to organize information, connect existing tools or simplify complex workflows, I'd be happy to explore the problem together.

Perhaps there is repetitive work that probably should not need repeating. Or the same information is being maintained in several places.

Perhaps existing tools should work together better. Or a standard solution gets almost all the way to what you need, but not quite.

Or perhaps you simply have a problem you do not yet know how to name.
